# Spatial Design 018 — Global multidisciplinary creator studio
Founder-approved direction: 1AGE Design serves all designers and creative entrepreneurs, including software, UI, 3D, gaming, fashion, clothing, merch, physical/industrial products, audio and visual design. Independent, walkable cosmic environment, five departments: global hub, digital design, product design, sound/media and creator studio. Reuses the established spatial foundation and local shared assets. Existing production and Godot design tools are not replaced.

Peer-to-peer platform direction: designer portfolios, discovery, collaboration, requests/briefs, commissions and licensing of original work, with shared Page identity. Design is the creative discovery and creation layer; Work handles employment and professional opportunities/contracts, Shop handles shared product commerce, Space manages virtual property, Games and World consume authorized assets, Quest provides community missions, and Exchange may support permitted settlement. Creators retain rights to their original work; license scopes, consent, attribution, moderation, dispute handling, secure identity and payment compliance are required before enabling real transactions or publishing. No mandatory blockchain knowledge or wallet. Blockchain may support authorized provenance and settlement later; it does not establish copyright by itself.

Current prototype includes searchable discipline categories and a memory-only project brief/portfolio composer. No real designer listings, uploads, modeling/editor tools, persistent profiles, public publishing, collaboration, contracts, licensing or payment yet. No existing assets or entry files changed. Browser/render/controller tests and actual prestige graphics validation pending. Do not merge or deploy without approval.
